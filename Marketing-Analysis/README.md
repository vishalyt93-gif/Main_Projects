# 📊 Marketing Analysis Dashboard

## 🚀 Project Overview
Built an interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze marketing performance, customer engagement, and customer feedback. The goal was to uncover actionable insights and support data-driven business decisions.


## 🎯 Business Objectives

### 📈 Increase Conversion Rates
Identify factors affecting conversion rates and detect where users drop off in the conversion funnel.

### 📱 Enhance Customer Engagement
Analyze which types of marketing content generate the highest customer engagement across social media channels.

### ⭐ Improve Customer Feedback Scores
Analyze customer reviews to identify recurring positive and negative themes for product and service improvement.


## 🛠️ Tools Used
- SQL
- Power BI

## 📌 Dashboard Features
✔️ Conversion Trends Analysis  
✔️ Social Media Engagement Analysis  
✔️ Customer Reviews Analysis  


## 💡 Key Insights

### 📈 Conversion Analysis
- Identified a consistent **3-year seasonal conversion pattern**, with conversion rates peaking in **January (up to 21%)** and recovering again in **December**, helping support campaign timing and product planning.

- In **2025**, conversion performance varied across months. **March** and **September** showed stronger product conversions, while **July** recorded the lowest conversion rate at **2.9%**. This highlights opportunities for targeted campaigns during lower-performing periods.

---

### 📱 Social Media Engagement
- Social media views peaked in **February** and **July (2024)**, indicating strong audience engagement during these periods.

- Engagement declined from **August 2024 onward** and continued into **2025**, suggesting reduced audience interaction and the need for content strategy optimization.

---

### ⭐ Customer Review Analysis
- Customer reviews was largely positive, with **154 four-star reviews** and **138 five-star reviews**.

- Lower ratings were less frequent, with only **27 one-star reviews** and **60 two-star reviews**, indicating overall customer satisfaction while still highlighting areas for improvement.
